# pilo.trainer.mlt - Core Source Library

A curated collection of real, verified resources for building expertise in machine learning and large language models. Organized by topic area for structured learning paths.

## Preference Alignment

| Title | Author / Organization | URL | Description |
|-------|----------------------|-----|-------------|
| RLHF: Training Language Models with Human Feedback | Hugging Face | https://huggingface.co/blog/rlhf | Comprehensive blog post explaining the RLHF pipeline: supervised fine-tuning, reward modeling, PPO training, and practical implementation details. |
| Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) Paper | Rafael Rafailov et al. (Stanford) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290 | Paper introducing DPO: aligning language models using preference data without reward models or RL. Simpler and more stable than PPO-based RLHF. |
| DeepSeekMath GRPO Paper | DeepSeek AI | https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03300 | Paper introducing Group Relative Policy Optimization. GRPO simplifies PPO by using group-relative advantage estimation without a critic model. |
| Sebastian Raschka - RLHF Overview | Sebastian Raschka | https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/llm-alignment-and-rlhf | Article covering the full alignment pipeline: SFT, reward modeling, RLHF with PPO, DPO, and practical comparisons of alignment techniques. |
| Constitutional AI | Anthropic | https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073 | Paper on self-supervised alignment using AI-generated feedback guided by a set of principles. Foundation for Anthropic's Claude models. |
| Zephyr: Direct Distillation of LM Alignment | Hugging Face H4 Team | https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16944 | Paper on training Zephyr-7B using DPO on curated preference data. Demonstrates that small models can achieve strong alignment. |

## Quantization

| Title | Author / Organization | URL | Description |
|-------|----------------------|-----|-------------|
| llama.cpp | Georgi Gerganov | https://github.com/ggerganov/llama.cpp | C/C++ inference engine with GGUF quantization (Q2_K through Q8_0). CPU inference, Metal, CUDA, and Vulkan support. The standard for local LLM inference. |
| GPTQ | Elias Frantar et al. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17323 | Paper on post-training quantization for transformers. One-shot weight quantization to 4-bit and 3-bit with minimal quality loss. Basis for AutoGPTQ. |
| AutoGPTQ | AutoGPTQ contributors | https://github.com/AutoGPTQ/AutoGPTQ | Easy-to-use library for GPTQ quantization. Integrates with Hugging Face Transformers for quantizing and running quantized models. |
| AWQ: Activation-aware Weight Quantization | Ji Lin et al. (MIT) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00978 | Paper on quantization that protects salient weights based on activation magnitudes. Better quality than GPTQ at 4-bit. |
| SmoothQuant | Guangxuan Xiao et al. (MIT) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.10438 | Paper on W8A8 quantization: shifts quantization difficulty from activations to weights via mathematically equivalent transformations. |
| ExLlamaV2 | turboderp | https://github.com/turboderp/exllamav2 | High-performance inference library for quantized LLMs. Supports EXL2 quantization format with mixed precision per-tensor for optimal quality. |
